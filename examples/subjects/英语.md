## 一、单词拼写（每空1分，共10分）

1. The first day at senior high left a deep ____________ (印象) on me.
2. She is ________________ (好奇的) about everything new in senior high school.
3. We need to ________________ (申请) for the student club before Friday.
4. He tried to ________________ (探索) different ways to solve the problem.
5. You should ________________ (组织) your time well at senior high.

## 二、词性转换（每空1分，共10分）

1. curious → ____________ (n.)
2. impress → ____________ (n.)
3. organize → ____________ (n.)
4. argue → ____________ (n.)
5. confident → ____________ (n.)

## 三、读后续写微写作

> 对照：He turned his head toward the door, his hands trembling, his heart pounding. | 他转头看向门口，双手发抖，心怦怦直跳。

- 动作链：turn one's head toward → catch sight of → freeze on the spot
- 情绪链：heart pounding → a lump in one's throat → breathe a sigh of relief

## 四、话题评测

1. What matters most in choosing an academic pathway?

A. Personal interest　B. Parents' advice　C. School ranking　D. Future salary

2. Which of the following is the author's attitude toward school clubs?

A. Doubtful　B. Supportive　C. Indifferent　D. Critical

---

## 参考答案

1. impression　2. curious　3. apply　4. explore　5. organize
词性转换：curiosity / impression / organization / argument / confidence
话题评测：1. A　2. B
